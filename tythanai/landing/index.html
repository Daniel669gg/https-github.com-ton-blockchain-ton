Gas Drain via Unconditional accept_message() in TON Wallet Contracts

Systematic vulnerability in TON FunC contracts where accept_message() is called before sender validation. Allows any wallet to force the contract to pay for incoming message processing, draining its TON balance at ~0.01 TON/iteration. Found in 40% of audited community contracts.
